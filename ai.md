# RT Digital Marketing AI Platform

## Project Vision

We are building a new AI-powered digital marketing platform from scratch.

The platform will help marketing professionals, companies, and factories understand their advertising performance, make better marketing decisions, and improve sales.

The platform will use specialized AI agents to research, analyze data, generate insights, and recommend practical actions.

## Main Mission

Build an intelligent marketing system that can:

1. Analyze digital advertising campaigns.
2. Understand campaign performance and customer behavior.
3. Discover successful marketing strategies.
4. Identify problems and opportunities.
5. Recommend improvements based on real data.
6. Help create better advertising content.
7. Support business growth through data-driven decisions.
